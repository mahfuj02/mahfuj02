### 👋 About me

I'm a full stack developer living in Winnipeg. Most of my work is in **TypeScript, Next.js and Node.js**, and I like building the whole thing myself — the database, the API, and the screens people actually use.

Lately I've been building a lot with AI. [RoleReady](https://role-ready-snowy.vercel.app) is an interview practice app where you answer out loud and get feedback from AI. I also just enjoy playing with new AI tools to see what they can do.

I've written **C++** for years through competitive programming (1000+ problems solved), and I recently built an AV Device Controller to get into embedded-style programming. Right now I'm learning **C# and .NET**.

I studied Full Stack Web Development at **Red River College Polytechnic**. Before that, I worked at **LIILAB** in Bangladesh as a junior software engineer, where I led a team and worked between the backend and WordPress teams.

📫 Open to full-stack and software developer roles — feel free to reach out.
